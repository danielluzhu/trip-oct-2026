Know before you go

RoadsThe Sun Road is closed from Avalanche Creek to Logan Pass (snow, since Oct 9), so no Logan Pass or Highline. The Two Medicine Road is closed at Running Eagle Falls for construction. Text GNPROADS to 333111 for live status.
BearsBears are feeding hard before winter. Carry bear spray where you can reach it, hike in a group and make noise on blind corners.
HuntingHunting seasons are open on national forest land outside the park, including along the Middle Fork. Wear something bright off park trails.
Cold waterThe Middle Fork is snowmelt-cold in October. Layers that stay warm wet (no cotton) and a dry change of clothes in the car.
Fall colorWestern larch turn gold mid to late October; look for them on the hillsides around Lake McDonald and the North Fork.
SunUV stays moderate this late in the year, but it bounces off snow and water. Sunglasses and sunscreen for the float and anything above treeline.
DaylightAbout 10h 45m of light. Start long hikes by 9 AM so you're off the trail before the ~6:45 PM sunset.
