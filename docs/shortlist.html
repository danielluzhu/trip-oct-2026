Montana won

Kept for the record — and because one of these is the next trip

Five places went through the same questions: what does it cost to fly 3 people from New York, 2 from the Bay Area and 1 from Seattle; what is the weather actually doing in the third week of October; what does a four-bedroom cabin run; and what is there to do once you land. Glacier took it on the strength of the arrival — 19 minutes from plane to town — and on having a nonstop from two of the three origins. Everything below is what the other four looked like.
