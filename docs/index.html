Both NYC nonstops are summer-only and finished in September. Connect via Denver, Chicago or Minneapolis — about 7h50 door to door at best.

United's SFO–FCA nonstop runs May 21 through Oct 24, one flight a day, so Oct 16-19 clears it by five days. Earlier reporting had this route dying around Columbus Day; the published range says otherwise.

Top things to do
  • Avalanche Lake and the Trail of the Cedars
  • Lake McDonald — and the golden eagle migration overhead
  • Middle Fork of the Flathead, floating and fishing into November
  • Western larch turning gold across the valley
  • Many Glacier — Grinnell Lake, Redrock Falls, Apikuni Falls
  • Whitefish town: breweries, distillery, and a real main street
  • National Bison Range, Red Sleep Mountain Drive (through Oct 31)
Cheapest week of the year to be here and you'll have it nearly to yourself — but Logan Pass very likely closes on or before your dates, and every in-park lodge and guided outfitter has already shut.
