The plan

Four nights in Whitefish

Based in Whitefish · Thu Oct 15 - Mon Oct 19, 2026 · 4 nights

Friday: arrive and stock up. Saturday: Apgar Lookout. Sunday: float the Middle Fork. Logan Pass will likely be closed and nothing in the park serves food.

Day 1 · Fri 10/16 (Arrival)

Arrive, stock up, short walk.
  1. Midday
    Land at FCA, collect two AWD SUVs ~$800 for both cars, 4 days at ~$100/day
    FCA is 19 min from Whitefish. Rental desks are by baggage claim.
  2. First thing
    Text GNPROADS to 333111
    Live Going-to-the-Sun Road status.
  3. ~1 PM
    Costco, 345 Cascade Loop, Kalispell
    The new location, 17 min from Whitefish. Buy lunches for the park.
  4. Late afternoon
    Check in, short walk
    Lion Mountain loop, ~3 flat miles, 5 min from town. Or Whitefish City Beach.
  5. Dinner
    Dinner in town
    Craggy Range (burgers, $14-19) or Tupelo Grille ($34-57, 20% auto-gratuity for 6+).
  6. After dinner
    Movie: Brokeback Mountain
    Pack for tomorrow. Out by 7:30.

Day 2 · Sat 10/17 (The big hike)

Apgar Lookout, then Lake McDonald.
  1. 7:30 AM
    Out of the house
    West Glacier gate is 39 min. Trailhead is a couple of miles past it.
  2. At the gate
    Entrance fee $70
    $35 per vehicle, so $70 for two.
  3. The big one
    Apgar Lookout — 7.2 miles round trip, ~1,800 ft bring lunch
    Switchbacks to a fire lookout over Lake McDonald. 4-5 hours with lunch.
  4. The split
    Easier option
    Second group takes the other SUV to Trail of the Cedars (0.7 mi) and Avalanche Lake (4.6 mi round trip). Meet at Lake McDonald Lodge.
  5. If the gate has moved down
    If the road closes lower
    If the road closes below Avalanche: Johns Lake (3 mi) or Rocky Point (1.1 mi).
  6. Bring it with you
    No food in the park
    Every in-park restaurant is closed. Bring food and extra water.
  7. Late afternoon
    Lake McDonald
    Golden eagle migration over Mount Brown, visible from the lodge.
  8. Evening
    Dinner in Whitefish
    Book dinner ahead.

Day 3 · Sun 10/18

Float the Middle Fork.
  1. The main event
    Float the Middle Fork — four boats ~$2,600 + tips
    Great Northern Resort: $650 full day or $490 half, 2 anglers per boat.
  2. Before you go
    Fishing licences $32 pp
    Montana licence needed on the Middle Fork. Buy at ols.fwp.mt.gov before Sunday.
  3. If nobody wants a boat
    Bison Range or a day in town
    Red Sleep Mountain Drive (through Oct 31, $35 per van; call 406-644-2211 an hour ahead), or breweries and the distillery in Whitefish.
  4. What not to do today
    Many Glacier
    2h50 each way with Logan Pass closed. Skip it.
  5. Evening
    Theatre in Bigfork
    'The Game's Afoot' at Bigfork Center for the Performing Arts, 40 min away.
  6. Before bed
    Pack
    Checkout Mon 10/19. FCA is 19 min away.
