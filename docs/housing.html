Booking notes

  • Your weekend sits in a genuine trough. MSU plays away Oct 17 (home games are Oct 24 and 31), and Montana's general rifle season opens Oct 24 — five days after you leave. One Paradise Valley property runs $527/night on Oct 9-12 and $345 on Oct 23-26; you're in between.
  • Fees are brutal on a short stay. Cleaning alone runs $240-680 per booking for large Montana properties, which on 3 nights is 15-30% of the nightly subtotal before the platform fee and tax land on top. Budget +40-55% over the headline rate.
  • Montana charges 8% lodging tax statewide. Big Sky and West Yellowstone add a 4% resort tax on top (12% total). Bozeman, Livingston, Ennis and Gardiner are 8%.
  • October discounts are not uniform. Paradise Valley runs ~57% under July on the same properties; Ennis barely moves because most homes post flat year-round rates.
  • Big Sky is the most expensive of the six in absolute terms despite being in its own cheapest month — and it's half-shuttered, with lifts closed from Sept 13 until Nov 25.
  • Anything sleeping 10 usually means 4-5 bedrooms; Airbnb's guest count includes sofa beds, so read the actual bed list.
  • Snow is normal by mid-October and nights sit near freezing. Get a plowed driveway and skip anything up a forest road.
