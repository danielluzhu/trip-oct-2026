Booking notes

  • Mid-October is genuinely shoulder season: Bozeman market ADR runs ~$305 vs $325 in September, and hotels sit 40-50% under July peaks.
  • MSU Bobcats play away on Oct 17, so no home-game lodging spike. The following weekend (Oct 24, Weber State) is a home game and would cost more.
  • Montana charges 8% lodging tax statewide; Big Sky and West Yellowstone add a 4% resort tax on top (12% total). Bozeman, Livingston and Ennis are 8%.
  • Big Sky is the cheapest headline rate but half-shuttered in mid-October — lifts closed, many restaurants dark. Cheap for a reason.
  • Anything sleeping 10 usually means 4-5 bedrooms; Airbnb's guest count includes sofa beds, so read the actual bed list.
  • Snow is normal by mid-October (6 inches is the October average) and every night is near freezing. Get a plowed driveway; skip anything up a forest road.
  • Yellowstone's interior roads stay open to cars until early November, so the park is still drivable on these dates. Beartooth Highway usually closes mid-October.
