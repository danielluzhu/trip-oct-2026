House guide

Spring Creek at the Quarry, managed by Vacay Whitefish. From their guest guide.
Getting in
Door codes aren't on the public site. Get them from Dan or the guide link Vacay texted him.
  • Enter through the garage: type the garage keypad code and press Enter.
  • The inside door from the garage unlocks with its own code on its keypad.
  • If the garage won't open (power outage, emergency), a lockbox just right of the garage and around the corner holds backup keys for the front door.
WifiNetwork "Vacay Whitefish Guest". No password: enter your name and email on the welcome page. The router is on the dresser in the main-floor bedroom.
Guest servicesVacay Whitefish, based in town: (406) 609-9595 (call or text), guest@vacaywhitefish.com, vacaywhitefish.com. No mid-stay cleaning.
EmergenciesCall 911 first, then Vacay. Fire extinguisher is under the kitchen sink. Power out in part of the house: check the breaker panel in the garage.
ParkingTwo vehicles max, and the garage counts. No street parking. Extra spots may be free at the clubhouse.
ClubhouseHot tub, pool and fire pit, open until 11 PM. The key fob hangs on the fridge ($225 if lost). Spa towels are in the basket in the entry closet; don't take the white bath towels, and bring the spa towels back.
Heat and fireNest thermostat by the front door runs heat and AC. Gas fireplace remote is on the wall left of the fireplace; only run it when someone's home.
Kitchen and grillKeurig (pods). Gas grill on the patio: turn the propane off after each use and scrape it clean when we leave. A backup tank is by the grill; text Vacay if both are empty.
BedsThe living-room sofa pulls out. Sheets and extra bedding are in the master bedroom closet.
TV and laundrySmart TVs with cable in the living room and every bedroom; log out of streaming before we leave. Washer and dryer: only run them while someone's home.
House rulesNo smoking ($500 minimum), no parties, quiet hours 10 PM to 8 AM, no music outside, no fireworks. Snow shovel provided; ski gear and snowshoes stay in the garage.
TrashEverything bagged, out to the community dumpster just north of the main entrance (first right as you enter the Quarry). $50 charge if trash is left behind.
PackagesDon't ship anything to the house.
Checkout, Mon 10/19
  • Out by 10 AM Monday (late checkout gets charged).
  • Fill and start the dishwasher.
  • Used towels, sheets and pillowcases on the bathroom floors. Leave mattress protectors, blankets and comforters on; don't make the beds.
  • Check closets and drawers (earbuds, chargers, sunglasses).
  • Log out of streaming on every TV.
  • Bag the trash and take it to the dumpster.
  • Tell Vacay about any damage.
