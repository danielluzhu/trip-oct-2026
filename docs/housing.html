Booking notes

  • Mid-October is shoulder season — between summer and ski — so this is the cheapest the area gets outside of April.
  • Snow is possible by mid-October at these elevations. Get a place with a plowed driveway and don't book anything up a forest road.
  • Anything sleeping 10 usually means 4-5 bedrooms; Airbnb's guest filter counts sofa beds, so check the actual bed list.
  • Book two vehicles rather than one — nothing at BZN seats 10, and the group will want to split up anyway.
  • Yellowstone's north entrance (Gardiner) stays open to cars year-round; most interior roads close to cars in early November.
