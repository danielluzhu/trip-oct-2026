Getting there

Seattle and SF get nonstops. NYC connects.
RouteAirlineMid-Oct
SEA → FCA Alaska, daily nonstop
Year-round, 1h25, roughly 106 flights a month. Comfortably the easiest origin.
EWR / LGA → FCA United / American no nonstop
Both NYC nonstops are seasonal and end in September. October is connecting-only, usually via Denver, Chicago or Minneapolis — about 7h50 door to door at best.
JFK → FCA — no nonstop
Has never had a nonstop in any season.
SFO → FCA United, seasonal nonstop
Alive for your dates: United runs it daily from May 21 to Oct 24, one flight a day, which leaves five days of margin after you fly home. It is still the last week of the season, so a schedule change would hit this before anything else.
OAK / SJC → FCA — no nonstop
No service. Allegiant dropped Oakland entirely. The Bay Area crew flies SFO, or considers Missoula.
The Missoula alternative (MSO)

Missoula is the second way into the Flathead: 137 miles south of Whitefish, about 2h45 up US-93 along the east shore of Flathead Lake, and not a mountain pass the whole way. It is a bigger, less seasonal airport than Kalispell, and on US DOT Q1 2026 fare data FCA averages about 18% above it. The trade is the drive — two of them, on arrival and departure days.

RouteAirlineMid-Oct
SEA → MSO Alaska, year-round nonstop
Roughly 105 flights a month, 27 departures a week. Seattle already has a year-round nonstop into Kalispell, so this one is only worth it on price.
SFO → MSO United, seasonal nonstop
Daily, and flying in October — but Missoula's own airport lists it as seasonal and no end date is published. United's other Montana seasonal routes stop Oct 24, so assume the same and check before you count on it.
EWR / JFK / LGA → MSO — no nonstop
No nonstop from any New York airport, in any season. The NYC three connect via Denver, Chicago, Minneapolis or Salt Lake — the same shape of day as flying into Kalispell, so Missoula buys them nothing but a cheaper fare and a 2h45 drive.
Worth pricing for the Bay Area two and the Seattle one if FCA fares spike; not worth it for the NYC three, who connect either way and would land on top of a long drive. Renting in Missoula also means returning the cars there — build the drive into both the arrival and the departure day.
Glacier Park International is a heavily seasonal airport and its own website is stale — it still advertises Allegiant Oakland service that no longer exists. The SFO nonstop clears your dates by five days, but it is the last week of the season, so a schedule trim would hit it before anything else. Housing
Cleaning is flat per booking, so on three nights it lands harder than a percentage would. Tax is 8% statewide; Whitefish adds 3% resort tax, which the area dropdown fills in.
Rental cars
Seven agencies sit in the FCA terminal by baggage claim, and October is the easy end of the year for availability. Take AWD anyway — valley roads will be bare, but nightly lows are already below freezing and the mountain roads are where you'd want it. Add 4% Montana rental vehicle tax plus airport concession fees. For 8 you need two vehicles; a full-size SUV realistically seats 7 before luggage.
Food & drink
Montana has no general sales tax. Whitefish's 3% resort tax hits restaurant meals and alcohol but exempts groceries — so cooking in is taxed at zero. Kalispell, Columbia Falls and Bigfork charge nothing on meals either. Costco moved: it's now 345 Cascade Loop, Kalispell, about 17 minutes from Whitefish. Watch for 20% auto-gratuity on parties of six or more at the upscale places, and confirm hours before you drive.
