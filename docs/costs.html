Listed as ending sometime in October with no published date. Seasonal FCA routes typically finish around Columbus Day (Oct 12), which would put your Oct 16 outbound just past it. Check united.com before counting on it.

No service. Allegiant dropped Oakland entirely. The Bay Area crew flies SFO, or considers Missoula.
Glacier Park International is a heavily seasonal airport and its own website is stale — it still advertises Allegiant Oakland service that no longer exists. If the SFO nonstop is dead, Missoula is a real hedge for the Bay Area three: cheaper fares, a year-round United SFO nonstop, and a 2h30 drive to Whitefish. Housing
A flat $350 cleaning fee, ~15% platform fee and Montana lodging tax (8%, or 12% in Big Sky & West Yellowstone) are added automatically. On three nights that lands around +45-55%.
Kayak trailing-route averages read Aug 2026, not quotes. October is actually the cheapest month on the NYC-Kalispell route (~$403). FCA averages ~18% above Missoula and ~27% above Spokane on US DOT Q1 2026 fare data.
Rental cars
Gas & a Yellowstone day trip:
Seven agencies sit in the FCA terminal by baggage claim, and October is the easy end of the year for availability. Take AWD anyway — valley roads will be bare, but nightly lows are already below freezing and the mountain roads are where you'd want it. Add 4% Montana rental vehicle tax plus airport concession fees. For 8 you need two vehicles; a full-size SUV realistically seats 7 before luggage.
Food & drink
Montana has no general sales tax. Whitefish's 3% resort tax hits restaurant meals and alcohol but exempts groceries — so cooking in is taxed at zero. Kalispell, Columbia Falls and Bigfork charge nothing on meals either. Costco moved: it's now 345 Cascade Loop, Kalispell, about 17 minutes from Whitefish. Watch for 20% auto-gratuity on parties of six or more at the upscale places, and confirm hours before you drive.
