Housing
A flat $325 cleaning fee, ~15% platform fee and Montana lodging tax (8%, or 12% in Big Sky & West Yellowstone) are added automatically. On three nights that lands around +45-55%.
Fares are Kayak trailing-12-month route averages retrieved Aug 2026 — indicative, not quotes for these dates. Oct 16-19 is a Fri-Mon weekend ~2 months out, so expect the upper half of each range.
Rental cars
Gas & a Yellowstone day trip:
BZN is roughly 2.4x Denver for car rental. Minivans are the single most overpriced class here ($148/day vs $109 for a full-size SUV) — take two SUVs instead. One 7-seat SUV will not fit 8 people plus luggage. The majors will not guarantee AWD: you book a class, not a drivetrain, so call the counter. Add ~30% for Montana's 4% vehicle tax, airport concession and license fees.
Food & drink
Bozeman has no sales tax on groceries or restaurant meals — menu price is the price. Restaurants run 25-50% above US average while groceries sit near par, so cooking is the real saving. Costco at 2505 Catron St is 10 min from the airport. For a party of 8, Little Star Diner and Yellowstone Valley Grill are the only two places that book 8-10 online; everywhere else wants a phone call.
