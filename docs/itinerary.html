The plan

Three nights at the edge of Glacier

Based in Whitefish / Flathead Valley · Fri Oct 16 - Sun Oct 19, 2026 (flexible, 3-5 days)

This is a shoulder-season trip, and the plan is built that way. Assume Logan Pass is closed — NPS shuts the alpine road around the third Monday of October, which is your checkout day, and in 2025 it actually closed on Oct 14. Every in-park lodge and restaurant shut in September, and the guided outfitters cluster-end on Oct 15, the day before you land. What's left is genuinely good: the lower west side is open and empty, the larch are turning, golden eagles are migrating over Lake McDonald, and the Middle Fork fishes into November.

BaseWhitefish — 19 min to the airport, 39 to the park gate
Weather54°F day, 29°F night. 1.1in of snow is the October normal in the valley

Park entry$35 per vehicle, 7 days. No vehicle reservations required in 2026
MovieBrokeback Mountain — shot an hour north over the Alberta line
The catchNothing in the park is open for food. Bring lunch every day
Book before you go
  • Text GNPROADS to 333111 before you finalise anything — it's the live road-status line and it decides the shape of the weekend.
  • Middle Fork float trips: Great Northern Resort (800-735-7897) at $650 a full day, or Wild River Adventures. Two anglers per boat, so eight people is four boats.
  • Montana fishing licences at ols.fwp.mt.gov — about $32 each. Needed for the Middle Fork even though fishing inside the park isn't licensed.
  • Two AWD SUVs at FCA. Seven agencies are in the terminal, and October is the quiet end of the year.
  • Restaurant reservations, and confirm hours first — you're arriving in the gap between summer and ski season and things close without warning.
  • National Bison Range: call 406-644-2211 about an hour ahead if you want the Red Sleep Mountain Drive.
Everything with a price or a date was verified against NPS, the operator's own site, or published fare and rate data in Aug 2026. Road status is the one thing nobody can verify in advance — it turns on the weather in the week you travel.

Day 1 · Fri 10/16 (Arrival)

Land, provision, and find out whether the pass is open.
  1. Midday
    Land at FCA, collect two AWD SUVs ~$1,350 for 4 days, all in
    Glacier Park International is 19 minutes from Whitefish — far and away the easiest arrival of anywhere you considered. Seven rental agencies sit right by baggage claim.
  2. First thing
    Text GNPROADS to 333111
    Live Going-to-the-Sun Road status. If the alpine section is somehow still open, drive it tomorrow before the weather decides otherwise — that possibility expires the moment it snows.
  3. ~1 PM
    Costco, 345 Cascade Loop, Kalispell
    Note the address — the old US-93 warehouse closed in Nov 2025 and this is the new one, about 17 minutes from Whitefish. Nothing inside the park sells food in October, so you're packing lunches every day. Groceries are untaxed even in Whitefish.
  4. Late afternoon
    Settle in, then walk out for dinner
    Whitefish is the only town in the valley with a real main street. Craggy Range does burgers at $13.50-18.50; Tupelo Grille is the upscale end at $34-57 — but it auto-applies 20% gratuity to parties of six or more and won't split a bill more than four ways.
  5. After dinner
    Movie night: Brokeback Mountain
    Filmed just over the Alberta line, an hour or so north of where you're sitting. Old Boy and Internal Affairs are better films and belong to a different trip.

Day 2 · Sat 10/17

The park day. Lower west side, which is open no matter what the road does.
  1. At the gate
    Entrance fee $70
    $35 per vehicle for seven days, so $70 for two. No vehicle reservation is needed in 2026 — that system is gone, replaced by timed parking at Logan Pass which you probably won't reach anyway. The fee drops to $25 on Nov 1, just after you leave.
  2. Morning
    Trail of the Cedars, then Avalanche Lake
    The signature short hike of the west side and the one everyone should do. Trail of the Cedars is a 0.7-mile accessible boardwalk loop; Avalanche Lake continues 2.3 miles one way for 500 feet of climb. In summer this trailhead is a zoo. In October it won't be.
  3. Bring it with you
    There is no food in the park
    Every in-park restaurant closed in September — Russell's on Sept 28, Ptarmigan on Sept 21, Two Dog Flats on Sept 13. NPS says the Apgar camp store 'may' be open. Do not count on it.
  4. Afternoon
    Lake McDonald, and look up
    NPS runs a Hawk Watch from late September through October and roughly 2,000 golden eagles pass Mount Brown in a season — observable from around Lake McDonald Lodge. It's the best-sourced wildlife opportunity of the trip, and nobody plans for it.
  5. If the gate has moved
    Johns Lake Loop and Rocky Point
    Both sit below the Lake McDonald Lodge gate, so they stay reachable wherever NPS puts the closure. Johns Lake is a 3-mile loop with 160 feet of climb; Rocky Point is 1.1 miles and nearly flat. Apgar Lookout is the harder option at 3.6 miles and 1,850 feet.
  6. Optional detour
    Kalispell Farmers Market
    Saturday 9:00-12:30, if anyone wants a slower morning before the park.

Day 3 · Sun 10/18

Fishing, or the long haul east. Pick one — you can't do both.
  1. The main event
    Float the Middle Fork — four boats ~$2,600 + tips
    Montana's western district season runs to Nov 30, so this is one of the very few guided things still operating. Great Northern Resort posts $650 for a full day and $490 for a half, two anglers per boat, which means four boats for eight of you. Wild River Adventures also runs into October.
  2. Before you go
    Licences, and a wrinkle $32 pp
    The park boundary on the Middle Fork is the high-water mark, so a guided float there does need a Montana licence even though fishing inside the park doesn't. About $32 each at ols.fwp.mt.gov.
  3. The alternative
    Many Glacier — but know what it costs you
    It's the best of the east side: Grinnell Lake is 3.4 miles nearly flat, Redrock Falls 1.8, Apikuni Falls 1 mile for 700 feet. But with Logan Pass shut you go the long way round via US-2 and Marias Pass — 131 miles and about 2h50 each way. That's close to six hours in the truck for a day hike.
  4. Also open
    National Bison Range
    Red Sleep Mountain Drive runs through Oct 31 and is $35 for a van of 10 or more. They ask you to call 406-644-2211 about an hour ahead. Roughly the same direction as Missoula, so it pairs badly with Many Glacier and well with a slow day.
  5. Evening
    Something is actually on
    'The Game's Afoot' runs Oct 16-25 at the Bigfork Center for the Performing Arts — a genuine rainy-day option for eight. The Vienna Boys Choir is in Kalispell at 7pm, which may or may not be the vibe.

Day 4 · Mon 10/19 (Departure)

The easiest departure of any option you looked at — the airport is 19 minutes away.
  1. Morning
    Breakfast in Whitefish, then out
    FCA is 19 minutes from town, so there is no dawn scramble. Leave an hour and you're comfortable.
  2. Today, in fact
    This is the day the road usually closes
    The third Monday of October is NPS's stated target for shutting the alpine section. If it's still open this morning, you're witnessing the last hours of the season.
  3. If there's time
    Whitefish town, or the lake
    The main street is walkable and the distillery and breweries are in it. West-side park waters stay open to private boats through Oct 31 if anyone wants Lake McDonald one more time.
  4. Worth knowing
    The larch peak a week after you leave
    Western larch turn gold across the valley in the second half of October, but the best sources put peak around Oct 20-31 — Kintla and Bowman closer to Oct 22-25. You'll catch maybe 60-85% of it. Bigfork's larch festival was Oct 10, which you also miss.
